01 / Collision & Body Repair

Collision & Body Repair

Parking-lot hits, fender benders, dented doors and cloudy headlights. Teo repairs the damage, welds where it is needed and refinishes the panel so you cannot tell where it happened.

01.01

Collision repair

From fender benders to parking-lot hits. Damaged panels are repaired or replaced, then prepped, painted and color-matched so the repair blends in.

Example: Nissan Silvia front-end repair

01.02

Bumper repair & custom fit

Cracked, scuffed or hanging bumper covers repaired and refinished. Aftermarket bumpers custom fit so the gaps line up, then painted to match.

Example: Honda S2000 front bumper custom fit & paint

01.03

Door repair

Dents, creases and damaged door skins repaired, filled, blocked straight and refinished.

Examples: 2005 Nissan Altima rear door, 2006 BMW 330i driver door

01.04

Scratch & dent repair

Keyed paint, door dings, scuffs and scrapes corrected and refinished so they are gone, not just covered.

Good for: the everyday damage you are tired of looking at

01.05

Headlight repair & refurbishing

Hazy, yellowed or damaged headlights repaired and refurbished for a clearer look and better light output.

Good for: oxidized lenses and front-end repairs

01.06

Welding

Welding for body and panel repairs, handled by an I-CAR certified welding and auto body tech.

Credential: I-CAR certified in welding & auto body repair

02 / Paint & Refinishing

Paint & Refinishing

Matching a factory color on one panel, or changing the color of the whole car. Every paint job starts with careful masking and prep, and finishes in the booth.

02.01

Paint matching

New paint matched to your existing color so repaired panels sit right next to the original finish.

Tip: a photo of the paint-code sticker in your door jamb helps

02.02

Full paint jobs & color changes

All-over paint jobs and complete color changes, masked, prepped and sprayed in the booth.

Examples: 2019 Honda Civic Hatchback color change, Nissan Hardbody paint job

02.03

Custom paint

Custom colors and finishes for builds that need to stand out, planned with you before the first coat goes on.

Example: 2019 Ford Mustang GT 5.0 paint & vinyl stripe

03 / Custom Body & Aero

Custom Body & Aero

Wide-body kits, aero parts and chrome deletes, fitted and finished by a shop that builds its own style. Bring the parts and the idea, and Teo will lay out the plan.

03.01

Body kits & wide-body installs

Body kits and wide-body fender flares fitted, bodyworked and painted so they look like they came from the factory that way.

Example: 2019 Ford Mustang GT 5.0 wide-body install

03.02

Spoilers, splitters & wings

Front splitters, trunk spoilers and wings installed straight, secure and finished to match.

Examples: 2019 Dodge Charger splitter & spoiler, a wing on a 350Z

03.03

Chrome deletes & trim

Chrome grilles, badges and trim blacked out or refinished for a cleaner, murdered-out look.

Example: 2022 Toyota Tundra TRD front grille chrome delete

04 / Restoration

Restoration

Classic trucks deserve more than a quick respray. Restoration work covers body repair, prep and refinishing, one panel at a time.

04.01

Classic truck & car restoration work

Body repair, prep and refinishing for classic trucks and older cars, done with patience and respect for the original lines.

Example: 1987 Chevy C10 Custom Deluxe repair

Service FAQ

Good questions.

More answers on the home page FAQ, or just call.

Do you only take insurance jobs?

No. We handle insurance claims and customer-pay repairs, from a single scratch to a full color change.

Can you fix a parking-lot hit without repainting the whole car?

In many cases, yes: the damaged panel is repaired and the paint is matched to the rest of the car. Teo will tell you what your car needs after seeing photos.

Do you do full color changes?

Yes. Color changes and all-over paint jobs are sprayed in the shop's paint booth, like the 2019 Honda Civic Hatchback color change in our portfolio.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the damage, parts availability and the paint work involved. Ask for a timeline when you get your estimate.
