FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We'll work with your insurance company on collision claims. Bring your claim number and your adjuster's contact info, and we'll keep you updated through the process.

How do I get an estimate?

Send photos of the damage through the photo estimate form on this site, or text them to (804) 852-2954. Teo will follow up with next steps; some damage needs an in-person look before it can be priced.

How much will my repair cost?

Every job is priced after we see the damage, because the cost depends on the panels, parts and paint involved. Sending photos is the fastest way to get a number.

What are your hours?

We're open 7 days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 8 AM to 5 PM, Wednesday 8 AM to 5:30 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM and Sunday 9 AM to 5:30 PM.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

5255 Hull Street Rd N #14, Richmond, VA 23224, on the Hull Street corridor in Southside near the Chesterfield line. The shop has a wheelchair-accessible entrance and parking.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Call or text (804) 852-2954 before you come so Teo can make time to look at your vehicle. Sending photos first is the quickest way to get started.

Can you match my car's factory paint?

Yes. Paint matching is part of every repair, so the new work sits right next to your original finish.

Do you do custom work like wide-body kits and spoilers?

Yes. Recent builds include a wide-body 2019 Ford Mustang GT 5.0, a front splitter and trunk spoiler on a 2019 Dodge Charger and a grille chrome delete on a 2022 Toyota Tundra TRD.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, plus NFC mobile pay (tap to pay with your phone).

Is Teo certified?

Yes. Owner Teo Vasquez is I-CAR certified in welding and auto body repair.
