About Teo Customs Paint & Body

A Southside Richmond paint and body shop with a builder's eye, run by owner Teo Vasquez.

The shop

Teo's name is on every panel.

Teo Customs is a paint and body shop on Hull Street Road in Southside Richmond, run by owner Teo Vasquez.

Teo is I-CAR certified in welding and auto body repair. The shop handles the everyday work, like parking-lot hits, dented doors and insurance collision repairs, alongside the builds people stop to look at: wide-body Mustangs, JDM classics like the Skyline GT-R and Silvia, color changes and a 1987 Chevy C10 brought back from primer.

It is the kind of shop where the owner is the one masking the car, laying the paint and answering the phone. That is why drivers keep coming back, and why the Google rating sits at a perfect 5.0.

How we work

Shop rules.

Four things that stay the same whether the job is a door ding or a full build.

  1. 01

    Show the work

    Every stage gets documented, from primer to final reveal, with hundreds of posts on Instagram. Nothing to hide.

  2. 02

    Keep you in the loop

    Especially on insurance claims, you'll hear what is happening with your car instead of wondering.

  3. 03

    Sweat the details

    Straight panels, even gaps and a paint match you cannot pick out. The small stuff is the job.

  4. 04

    Build it like it's ours

    Custom work gets planned together and finished like it is going to a show, even when it is going to work on Monday.
