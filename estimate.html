    Photo tips

    Photos that get a better estimate

    1. Step back for one shot of the whole car at an angle, so we can see where the damage is.
    2. Get close to the damage, straight on, then from the side so dents and creases show up.
    3. Shoot in daylight or good light, and wipe off dirt first if you can.
    4. For paint work, add a photo of the paint-code sticker, usually in the driver's door jamb.
